Before You Launch: A Small Business Website Handoff Checklist

By Leonard E. Moore · October 28, 2026

A website launch is a handoff of a working business tool. Before you share the link, make sure customers can understand the offer, contact the business and get a clear next step.

1. Make the offer easy to understand

Read the homepage on your phone. Can a new visitor tell who you help, what you offer and how to reach you? Use one clear headline and a primary action such as requesting a quote or booking a consultation.

2. Test the customer journey

Open every main navigation link. Submit a test inquiry and confirm it arrives in the correct inbox. Check phone and email links, booking destinations and confirmation messages. Remove placeholder text and buttons that lead nowhere.

3. Confirm ownership and access

The business owner should know who controls the domain, hosting and website accounts. Record renewal dates and recurring costs. Give each person their own account where supported, use appropriate permissions and enable multifactor authentication. Share access through secure invitations instead of putting passwords in a handoff document.

4. Review the mobile experience

Check text size, spacing, menu behavior and tap targets. Make sure images fit the screen and the main contact action remains easy to find. Use descriptive image text and labels that explain what each form field needs.

5. Prepare for maintenance

Agree on who updates content, reviews inquiries and maintains the website. Confirm the backup and recovery process where applicable. Keep a record of what is included in ongoing support and what requires a separate quote.

6. Hand over a simple operating guide

Include the public website address, account owners, renewal information, instructions for common edits and a support contact. Walk through one routine task together so the owner can confidently use the site.

Your launch-day checklist

  • The offer and contact details are accurate.
  • Forms and booking links have been tested.
  • Mobile navigation and page content are readable.
  • Domain, hosting and account ownership are documented.
  • Maintenance responsibilities and support scope are clear.

A thoughtful launch gives the owner a website they can operate and customers a clear path to taking action.
