Samples matter. Embroidery, patches, labels and appliqué-style decoration must match the real supplier offering.

04 / THE DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can I place an order now? +

Not through this preview. The sellable catalog will open only when production, pricing, shipping, payments and returns have been verified.

Is every varsity item true appliqué? +

No construction claim is made until the actual supplier method, blank and sample have been confirmed for a particular product.

Will merchandise arrive for my event? +

Fixed-date delivery must be assessed per order, including production and carrier schedules. We will not guarantee a date before supplier confirmation.

How will sizing and returns work? +

Approved listings will carry specific size charts and care instructions. Final shipping and return policies will be available before payment is enabled.
