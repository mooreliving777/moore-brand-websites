SAFETY FIRST / REMOTE ASSISTANCE

Your device.
Your decision.

Support intake has not been verified on the separate Wix site. Until there is a confirmed way to request help, use these precautions before any remote session.

  1. 01

    Start the request yourself

    Use a contact route you independently verify. Do not trust unexpected support pop-ups or unsolicited access codes.

  2. 02

    Confirm identity and scope

    Know who is helping, what work is proposed, and whether any fee applies before you agree.

  3. 03

    Keep control

    Never share account passwords or banking credentials. Only grant remote access with explicit consent and stay present.

  4. 04

    Close the session

    End access when finished and review what changed. Ask for a written summary of completed work.
