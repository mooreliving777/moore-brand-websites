03 / BEFORE CHECKOUT

Questions before your first order.

Read clear, pre-launch information on garment verification, supplier routing, shipping and returns before any Harsh Truth merchandise is sold.

Our planned production process

  1. Approve the real item: Confirm the blank manufacturer, materials, sizing, decoration method, artwork rights and supplier quote.
  2. Inspect samples: Verify placement, embroidery/print registration, seams, feel, wash performance and packaging.
  3. Route each variant once: Assign one approved production partner, then test order creation, tracking and customer notifications.
  4. Publish final terms: Make accurate shipping regions, processing times, taxes, return rules and customer-service information available before payment.
Important: Printful and Apliiq are proposed production partners, not active fulfillment connections. No current garment inventory, event delivery guarantee, or shopping cart is offered by this site.

Frequently asked questions

Can I place an order right now?

Not yet. This site presents brand direction and inquiries only. We will open real product listings after samples, production methods, checkout, and final store policies are approved.

Are the varsity concepts true appliqué?

Not necessarily. An artistic mockup does not establish manufacturing capability. Every finished construction claim must be verified for the actual selected garment.

How long will shipping take?

Processing times, shipping locations and carrier transit will be confirmed per product before purchases are accepted. We cannot promise delivery dates before supplier approval.

What is your refund and exchange policy?

The final policy will depend on the actual items and suppliers and will be displayed before checkout. Any applicable consumer rights will still apply.

Can I request merchandise for an upcoming event?

Yes, submit a request through our contact page with the event date, destination and approximate quantity. A quote or delivery commitment is not made before verification.
