# Ten-section landing-page standard — Moore Brand Network

**Status:** proposed for review on November 7, 2026. Applies to all 13 GitHub Pages **companion** websites, not to the original WordPress properties or Shopify checkout.

## The conversion journey
1. **Hero:** brand-specific outcome, original artwork, one main next step.
2. **Social proof / verified context:** show traceable published sources. Never invent customer logos, ratings or use counts.
3. **Problem:** explain the actual visitor frustration using `data/landing-copy.json`.
4. **Solution:** concise brand-specific approach with a source-linked action.
5. **Features:** existing three original-site service or project links.
6. **How it works:** explore → verify current details → act through the appropriate source.
7. **Testimonials:** real permissioned feedback only. In the absence of it, use an honest explanation, not fake quotes.
8. **Pricing & availability:** only verified packages, or make unconfirmed status clear. City Web Design Pros has a conditional $99 rescue offer, not a guaranteed checkout. Other brands may require inquiry.
9. **FAQ:** clarify the companion nature, actions and payment boundaries.
10. **Final CTA:** existing original-site signup, contact, booking or content link; the companion collects no information.

Optional published journal content is placed between sections 8 and 9 and does not replace an essential step.

## How to edit
- Adjust brand-specific problem, solution and pricing/availability copy in `data/landing-copy.json`.
- Sections are generated by `scripts/landing_sections.py` and injected by `scripts/build.py`.
- Style in `assets/landing-sections.css`, inheriting each brand's palette from `assets/network.css`.
- Rebuild with `python scripts/build.py`; check with `python scripts/check_landing.py`.
- Review pages at 320 px, 375 px, tablet and desktop widths; verify native FAQ toggles, keyboard focus, images, link targets and source claims.
- The generator maintains published static brand pages; do not hand-edit them without changing their generator.

## Review gates
- No new customer names, reviews, partner endorsements or statistics without verification/consent.
- No invented prices, stock, event dates, product availability or signup status.
- No newsletter forms, checkout, payment data collection, external scripts, paid APIs or production account settings on companion pages.
- Never change the original WordPress website, store, personal account or domain as a side effect.
- Existing draft PR #2 changes newsletter routing separately. Coordinate the review/merge sequence to avoid overwriting its changes.
- Do not merge or publish before explicit approval of the finished result.
